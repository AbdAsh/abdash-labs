The apps

Recto Multi-document notebooks where every answer names the document it came from. AskSheet Ask a spreadsheet in English. The data never leaves your browser — check the network tab. Critiq SEO and answer-engine review of any URL. The self-audit of abdash.net is not flattering. RAG Lab Which chunking actually wins? Benchmarks, not vibes — and the expensive model lost. GraphRead A document as an entity graph. Click any edge for the sentence that justified it. PlaneMode A language model running entirely in your browser. Turn on airplane mode and keep going.
